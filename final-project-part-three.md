# Changes made since Part II

Following the interviews and reflection process in Part II, I have made several improvements to my final design:

- Several Pittsburgh-specific sections are added, including the preface which states the abundance of Chinese restaurants in the CMU area, the distribution of Chinese restaurants in Pittsburgh, as well as my personal recommendation list of eateries;
- All data sources are reexamined, with more solid immigration and restaurant data from the Western PA Data Center, South Connecticut State University and the US Census Bureau being included into data charts;
- Exploring more diversified data charts like a live map enabled by Tableau's strong geospatial functionalities.

On the other hand, compromises had to be made elsewhere. Due to limitations in project time and software capabilities, I had to scrap the idea of using a pictorial unit chart or a dot chart to depict the growth of restaurants over time, and chose a simple stacked bar chart instead. After all, I believe that what matters most is not the variety of chart types, but rather the effectiveness of the ideas conveyed.
